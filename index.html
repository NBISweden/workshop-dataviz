Advanced Data Visualization

NBIS Workshop

Publication-ready Data Visualization using R including Quarto and Shiny

Beginner Level

  • Handle data in R for visualizations
  • Apply ggplot grammar to get publication-grade plots
  • Combine different data and/or different plots
  • PCA, Heatmaps, Phylogenetic trees and Maps

Advanced Level

  • Using the language of graphics: ggplot2
  • Basic and advanced plotting in ggplot2
  • Single-cell data visualizations with Seurat
  • Quarto notebooks and reports
  • Dynamic plotting with Quarto
  • Interactive web apps with Shiny

ggplot2

  • How to wrangle data correctly for ggplot
  • Working with geoms, aes, facets …
  • Combine different plots to publication-grade
  • PCA, Heatmaps and single-cell data visualization
  • Maps and phylogenetic trees visualizations (for interested)

Quarto

  • Quarto syntax and functions
  • Dashboards with quarto
  • Dynamic plotting with plotly, crosstalk and others

Shiny

  • UI and Server
  • Reactivity and error validations
  • Modularization and deployment

Learning outcomes

After this workshop, you should be able to:

Beginner level

  • Handle data in R for visualizations
  • Apply the grammar efficiently in ggplot to obtain the desired plot
  • Combine different data and/or different plots that are of publication-grade

Advanced level

  • Handle data in R for visualizations
  • Familiarize with ggplot2 functions and techniques at advanced level
  • Combine different data and/or different plots that are of publication-ready
  • Create interactive documents with Quarto for data exploration and analysis
  • Write your own simple Shiny app in R

Who can apply?

Eligibility

  • Audience: PhD students, postdocs and researchers in life sciences, healthcare and related fields
  • Background: Basic R experience needed
  • Eligibility: Swedish applicants are prioritized; international applicants are welcome
  • Research: To get the most from the workshop, one could try visualizing their own data during the course and we can help you.

How selection works

  • Criteria: Entry requirements, motivation to attend, and gender and geographical balance are considered when applications exceed capacity
  • Priority: PhD students are prioritized, followed by postdocs, PIs and other researchers
  • Master’s students: Accepted when seats are available
  • International applicants: Accepted when seats are available

NBIS training events do not provide formal university credits. If credits are essential, consult your home department before applying to establish whether it can recognize the workshop for credits. Participants with full attendance receive a certificate of participation stating the topics covered and the duration of the workshop.
